SAINTS & DRAGONS — Batch

Make a copy of this document for each batch and rename it, e.g. Batch — Sep 28 to Oct 8.

How to use this

  • Fill in one NIGHT section per card. There are five below; delete any you don't need, or copy one to add more.
  • Write only after the bold labels. Leave the labels exactly as they are, because that's how I find each field.
  • Grey italic lines are hints. You can leave them in; I ignore them.
  • Leave a field blank and I'll draft it for your approval, never ship it unseen. Everything you do write ships word for word. If something looks like a slip, I'll ask before changing it.
  • When the nights are marked Ready, send me the link. Before anything goes live, I'll show you each receipt as free readers and as paid readers will see it.

Pick lists

Virtue: Courage · Obedience · Mercy · Honesty · Humility · Perseverance · Forgiveness · Faithfulness

Theme: Knights · Dragons · Forests · Castles · Princes and princesses · The sea

Ages: Ages 4–6 · Ages 7–9

Need a new era, kind, virtue or theme? Write it in Notes for Claude and I'll add it to the list.
