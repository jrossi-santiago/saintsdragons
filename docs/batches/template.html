SAINTS & DRAGONS — Batch

Fill in one night per section and send me the link. Paste your own text or just name what you want. I write everything else: the hook, the card's question, why it's ours and the prayer, the tags and the reading times. I show you every receipt before it goes live.

Grey lines are hints; leave them or delete them. Skip a night by leaving it empty. Make a copy of this doc for each batch.
