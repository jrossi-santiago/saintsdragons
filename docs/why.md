## How it sits with the rest

The positioning line in `README.md` ("Dads learning things worth knowing &
passing them on to their kids. History, faith and virtue, handed down rather
than explained.") is what the site says about itself. This is why it exists
and whose voice it is in: a dad who did not learn these stories himself and
is learning them now. The two agree, and this does not replace that line on
the site.

Use it as the test for anything new, whether it is a piece, a feature or a post:

- **It starts with the dad learning.** He is not an expert handing down
  what he already knew. He is catching up, and bringing other dads along.
  Copy that sounds like a professor or a publisher has drifted.
- **The subjects are the four he named:** saints, explorers, battles,
  virtue. Anything else has to earn its place next to them.
- **It is handed down at bedtime.** The history is for him, and the
  bedtime story is where it reaches the kids. If something never gets
  as far as a child, ask whether it belongs.
- **It is for other dads to use, every day.** It has to fit a real day:
  under 10 minutes for him, and one story that night.

Posts written from this live in `docs/social/`.
