# Posts for X: the journey, and the first two weeks

Drafts written from `docs/why.md`. Each block below is one post, and all of them
are under 280 characters. Anything in `[brackets]` is yours to fill in with
the real thing: a number, a screenshot, something your kid said. Never post
a number or a bedtime moment that didn't happen. The whole series works only
if it is true.

Put the site link in the **first reply**, not in the post. X shows posts
with links to fewer people.

Two rules from the site also apply here. Say *history* and *bedtime story*,
not "brief", "card" or "night". The only time claim is "under 10 minutes".

## Between milestones: today's example

Today (Sept 23) the site has Drake on the history side and The Three
Little Pigs at bedtime. Post this as a thread tonight. Attach the Drake
portrait from the piece to the first post.

```text
In 1577 an English captain left Plymouth with five small ships and 164 men.

He came home with one ship, 56 men, and so much Spanish silver the Queen paid off England's entire national debt with her share.

Spain called him a pirate. She knighted him. 🧵
```

```text
Why he went: the Pope had split the new world between Spain and Portugal. England got to watch Spanish silver sail home by the ton.

Elizabeth backed Drake quietly. On paper, a trade voyage. If he got caught, a pirate. If he won, he was hers.
```

```text
In Argentina he beheaded his co-commander for mutiny.

Then he told the crew that from now on, gentlemen would "haul and draw with the mariner."

Everyone pulls a rope.
```

```text
Storms scattered the fleet until only his flagship was left. He renamed her the Golden Hind.

In the Pacific he caught a treasure galleon the sailors called Cacafuego, roughly "Fire-Crapper."

26 tons of silver. Half a ton of gold.
```

```text
Every Spanish ship in the Pacific was hunting him, so he didn't go back the way he came.

North to California. Across the Pacific. Around Africa. Home.

His first question in England: is the Queen still alive? If she wasn't, he'd likely hang.
```

```text
The Spanish had a name for him: El Draque. The Dragon.

Tonight my kids get The Three Little Pigs, and one question at bedtime:

What's something that was worth taking your time over?
```
